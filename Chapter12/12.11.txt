What would happen if we called process as follows?
process(shared_ptr<int>(p.get()));

a new shared pointer is initalized with the underlying raw pointer p and is destroyed at the end of processes scope but the original pointer p
still thinks it owns that data and needs be destroyed as well since these two pointers do not know about each other (p.get() does not transfer ownership) and since 
this data was freed at the end of process p is now a dangling pointer.
